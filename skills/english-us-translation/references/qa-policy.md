# Final Bilingual QA Policy

Every translation must undergo a silent final bilingual QA before it is returned. Treat the source text as authoritative.

## 1. Completeness and meaning

Verify that:

- every substantive source element is represented;
- no unsupported target content has been added;
- actors, actions, objects, recipients, and relationships are preserved;
- negation, modality, obligation, permission, prohibition, possibility, conditions, exceptions, restrictions, comparisons, quantities, temporal relationships, causal relationships, and logical scope remain correct;
- there is no mistranslation, unjustified generalization, unjustified narrowing, or reinterpretation.

## 2. Terminology

Verify that all applicable approved terminology is used, mandatory and forbidden terminology constraints are respected, and terminology remains consistent throughout the translation.

## 3. Names, numbers, and protected content

Check personal and organization names, geographic names, titles, laws, regulations, standards, abbreviations, numbers, dates, percentages, currencies, measurements, units, references, product/model numbers, codes, identifiers, URLs, email addresses, placeholders, variables, and markup tags against the source.

## 4. Structure

Verify paragraphs, headings, labels, numbering, lists, meaningful capitalization, source order, document logic, and absence of unsupported structural additions.

## 5. Target-language quality

Verify:

- natural and idiomatic American English;
- grammar, syntax, word order, collocations, terminology, punctuation, typography, and vocabulary;
- consistent use of the requested en-US locale without unintended mixing with another English variety;
- preservation of source style, register, tone, and purpose;
- applicable rules from `editorial-preferences.md`, if present;
- applicable patterns from `translation-examples.md`, if present and compatible with higher-priority instructions.

## 6. Final output compliance

Before returning the result, verify that the translation is complete, confirmed issues have been corrected, no process commentary or unintended labels have been inserted, and only the corrected final translation is returned unless the user explicitly requested another output format.

## Correction rule

Correct actual errors and active-rule violations. Do not rewrite a correct translation merely because another valid wording is possible.
